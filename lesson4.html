Lesson 4

🌿 Products of Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants make their own food using light energy.

The main raw materials needed for photosynthesis are carbon dioxide and water.

Light energy is absorbed by chlorophyll.

🍬 Glucose

One important product of photosynthesis is glucose.

Glucose is a simple sugar that can be used by the plant to release energy through respiration.

Plants can also use glucose to make other substances.

🌾 Starch

Some of the glucose produced during photosynthesis is converted into starch and stored in the plant.

🧠 Remember:

Starch is a storage form of carbohydrate in plants.

🌱 Other Substances Made from Glucose

Glucose can be used to produce other important substances needed by plants.

For example, glucose can be used to make cellulose, which forms part of plant cell walls.

Glucose can also be combined with nitrate ions to make amino acids, which are used to make proteins.

💨 Oxygen

Oxygen is also released during photosynthesis.

The oxygen is released into the atmosphere and can be used by organisms for aerobic respiration.

📚 WASSCE Reminder

Main food product: Glucose

Storage carbohydrate: Starch

Gas released: Oxygen

Cellulose: Made from glucose and used in plant cell walls.

Proteins: Made from amino acids.
