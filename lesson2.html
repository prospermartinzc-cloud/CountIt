☀️ Factors Affecting Photosynthesis

The rate of photosynthesis is affected by several factors. The main factors include light intensity, carbon dioxide concentration and temperature.

💡 Light Intensity

Light provides the energy needed for photosynthesis. When light intensity increases, the rate of photosynthesis generally increases until another factor becomes limiting.

🌫️ Carbon Dioxide Concentration

Carbon dioxide is a raw material needed for photosynthesis. Increasing the concentration of carbon dioxide can increase the rate of photosynthesis until another factor becomes limiting.

🌡️ Temperature

Photosynthesis is controlled by enzymes, so temperature affects its rate.

At low temperatures, the rate of photosynthesis is slow. As temperature increases, the rate increases up to an optimum temperature.

At very high temperatures, enzymes may become denatured, causing the rate of photosynthesis to decrease.

🧠 WASSCE Reminder:

Light intensity → provides energy for photosynthesis.

Carbon dioxide → raw material for photosynthesis.

Temperature → affects enzyme activity involved in photosynthesis.

When one factor limits photosynthesis, increasing another factor may have little or no effect.
