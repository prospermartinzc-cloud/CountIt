🌿 Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants make their own food using light energy.

1. What Is Photosynthesis?

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants make food using light energy.

Plants use carbon dioxide from the air and water from the soil to produce glucose.

2. Where Does Photosynthesis Occur?

Most photosynthesis takes place in the leaves of green plants.

Leaves contain many cells with structures called chloroplasts.

3. The Role of Light

Light provides the energy needed for photosynthesis.

For most plants, the Sun is the main source of light energy.

4. The Role of Carbon Dioxide

Plants take in carbon dioxide from the atmosphere.

Carbon dioxide provides the carbon needed to make glucose.

5. The Role of Water

Plants absorb water from the soil through their roots.

The water is transported through the plant to the leaves.

6. The Role of Chlorophyll

Chlorophyll is the green pigment found in chloroplasts.

It absorbs light energy needed for photosynthesis.

7. Glucose

Glucose is one of the main products of photosynthesis.

Plants can use glucose for energy or convert it into other substances such as starch.

8. Oxygen

Oxygen is released as a product of photosynthesis.

Some of this oxygen enters the atmosphere.

9. The Photosynthesis Equation

Photosynthesis uses carbon dioxide and water in the presence of light and chlorophyll.

The products include glucose and oxygen.

🌱 Carbon dioxide + Water → Glucose + Oxygen

10. Why Photosynthesis Matters

Photosynthesis is important because it produces food that supports plants and other living organisms.

It also releases oxygen into the atmosphere.
