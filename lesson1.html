🧬 Biology as a Science of Life

Welcome to your first CountIt lesson! In this lesson, you will discover what Biology is, what makes something living, and why Biology is important.

1. What is Biology?

Biology is the branch of science that studies living organisms and life.

The word Biology comes from two Greek words: bios, meaning life, and logos, meaning study.

2. Biology as a Science

Biology is a science because biologists use observation, evidence, experiments, measurements and logical reasoning to study living things.

Scientists do not depend only on opinions. They collect evidence and use it to reach conclusions.

3. What is Life?

Life refers to the condition that distinguishes living organisms from non-living things.

Living organisms carry out several important life processes that allow them to survive, grow and reproduce.

4. Characteristics of Living Organisms

Living organisms show several characteristics. A common way of remembering them is MRS GREN.

M — Movement

R — Respiration

S — Sensitivity

G — Growth

R — Reproduction

E — Excretion

N — Nutrition

5. Living and Non-living Things

Living organisms carry out life processes. Examples include humans, plants, animals, fungi and microorganisms.

Non-living things do not carry out all the characteristics of life.

For example, a car can move, but movement alone does not make it a living organism.

6. How Biology is Studied

Biologists use scientific investigation to understand life.

They may observe organisms, ask questions, collect information, carry out experiments, record results and draw conclusions.

7. Importance of Biology

Biology helps us understand living organisms and the world around us.

It contributes to areas such as medicine, agriculture, environmental protection, biotechnology and conservation.

8. Careers Related to Biology

Biology can lead to many areas of study and work, including medicine, nursing, pharmacy, veterinary science, agriculture, environmental science, biotechnology and biological research.

9. Biology in Everyday Life

Biology is part of everyday life.

Understanding exercise, nutrition, disease, plants, animals, food production and the environment all involve Biology.
