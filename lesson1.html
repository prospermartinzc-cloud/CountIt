Lesson 1: Habitat

What is a habitat?

A habitat is the natural place where an organism lives, obtains food, reproduces, and finds protection.

🌾 Savannah Habitat

A savannah is a habitat made up mainly of grasses with scattered trees.

🌳 Forest Habitat

A forest is a habitat dominated by many trees and other plants.
