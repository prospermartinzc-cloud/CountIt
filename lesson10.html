Lesson 10

🧂 Mineral Nutrition in Plants

Plants need certain mineral ions from the soil for healthy growth and development.

Mineral ions are absorbed mainly by the roots and are transported around the plant through the xylem.

🌱 Essential Mineral Ions

Different mineral ions have different functions in plants.

1. Nitrate ions

Nitrate ions are needed for the production of amino acids.

Amino acids are used to make proteins, which are needed for growth and repair.

2. Magnesium ions

Magnesium ions are needed to make chlorophyll.

Chlorophyll absorbs light energy needed for photosynthesis.

3. Phosphate ions

Phosphate ions are important for healthy root development and are also involved in energy-transfer processes.

⚠️ Mineral Deficiency

When a plant does not receive enough of an essential mineral ion, it may show deficiency symptoms.

For example, a lack of nitrate ions can cause poor growth and yellowing of older leaves.

A lack of magnesium can cause yellowing of leaves because less chlorophyll can be produced.

🧠 WASSCE Reminder:

Nitrate ions → amino acids → proteins → growth.

Magnesium ions → chlorophyll → photosynthesis.

Phosphate ions → healthy root development and energy-related processes.

Mineral deficiency → abnormal growth or visible symptoms in plants.
